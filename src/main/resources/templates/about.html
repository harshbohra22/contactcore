Our Story

Started in a small garage in 2020, ContactCore was born from a simple frustration: contact management shouldn't be boring or complicated. We set out to build a tool that feels as good to use as it looks.

Today, we're a team of passionate designers and engineers dedicated to crafting the most intuitive and beautiful contact management experience on the web.
